ADSA is a proposed Autonomous Data Science Assistant designed to guide users through data preparation, exploration, baseline machine learning and reporting in one connected workspace.

Explore the concept
The product

Meet ADSA

ADSA aims to make structured-data analysis easier to follow. It combines common early-stage data science tasks while keeping users informed and in control.

Data preparation

Upload CSV or Excel files, inspect data types, missing values and duplicates, and review suggested cleaning actions.

Exploratory analysis

View descriptive statistics, distributions, correlations and selected visualizations.

Model evaluation

Compare baseline models with task-appropriate metrics and visible limitations.

Reports and exports

Prepare downloadable summaries, charts and cleaned datasets for review.

How it works

A guided analysis workflow

01 · Upload

Start with a supported CSV or XLSX dataset.

02 · Review

Inspect data quality and approve transformations.

03 · Explore

Understand distributions, relationships and patterns.

04 · Evaluate

Compare suitable baseline models and review results.

05 · Export

Download outputs and share an analytical summary.

Who it is for

Practical learning and analysis

ADSA is intended for university students, beginner analysts, educators and small teams working with structured datasets. The initial concept prioritizes learning, transparency and first-pass analysis rather than high-stakes automated decisions.
