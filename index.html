Meet the product

One connected journey from dataset to decision.

ADSA is designed to bring essential early-stage data science tasks into one guided workspace — without hiding the reasoning behind the results.

▤

Data preparation

Inspect data types, missing values and duplicates, then review suggested cleaning actions before applying them.

⌁

Explore patterns

Understand distributions, descriptive statistics, correlations and useful visual summaries.

◫

Evaluate models

Compare baseline machine-learning models with metrics selected for the task at hand.

↗

Share insights

Prepare clear reports, charts and cleaned datasets that make analytical work easier to communicate.

The workflow

From raw file to a clearer picture.

A simple, reviewable process keeps users involved at every important stage.

Upload

Start with a supported CSV or Excel dataset.

Review

Check missing values, data types and potential issues.

Explore

Discover patterns through statistics and visualizations.

Evaluate

Compare baseline models and inspect their metrics.

Export

Take your cleaned data and findings with you.

Designed with purpose

Automation should make analysis clearer — not more mysterious.

ADSA is envisioned as an assistant, not an unquestioned decision-maker. The goal is to help users understand what happened to their data and why an analytical result matters.

Our approach

Keep people in the loop at every step.

Useful automation works best when users can inspect, question and approve the process.

  • Review suggested data-cleaning actions.
  • Understand model metrics and limitations.
  • Keep evaluation steps transparent and reproducible.
  • Export results for further review and communication.
Who it's for

Built around the people working with data.

The initial concept focuses on common structured-data workflows for learners and small teams.

Students & learners

Follow a guided process while learning how data preparation, exploration and modeling fit together.

Beginner analysts

Move through routine dataset checks and first-pass analysis in a connected workspace.

Educators & small teams

Make analytical workflows easier to demonstrate, discuss and communicate.
